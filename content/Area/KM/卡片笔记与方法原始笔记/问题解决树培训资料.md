- Module Objectives

  - By the end of this module, the participant will be able to:

    - Understand the benefits of Problem Solving Trees

    - Understand the concept of Problem Solving Trees

    - Understand how to construct a Problem Solving Tree

    - Read and interpret Problem Solving Trees
- Problem Solving Trees Why Highly Recommended

  - Why would you want to use a Problem Solving Tree?

    - Major problem has occurred

    - High visibility

    - Daily updates

    - 30+ people on conference calls including customer multiple times per day

      - Wide range of area of experience for attendees

      - Each firing rapid random questions related to:

        - 🗝What do we know?

        - 🗝Could it be this or that?

        - 🗝 Could it be tied into this problem?

        - 🗝When did it start?

        - 🗝Who is affected?

        - 🗝What products are affected?

        - 🗝 Where is it occurring?

        - 🗝 Is it the hardware, software or vehicle?
  - Close your eyes! – Put yourself in position of problem solving leader
